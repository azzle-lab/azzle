# V2 griefing resistance

V2 bounds rather than eliminates griefing. Task duration is capped at 30 days; claimed funding is limited to one day; delivered work has bounded dispute/default windows; disputes have absolute evidence-plus-ruling cutoffs; arbitration assignment reserves bond capacity; task exposure has global and 20% per-poster USD6 caps; gateway input and deadline windows are capped; public scope is write-once and size-limited.

Accepted trade-offs remain: a capitalized sybil can occupy part of open-task capacity and later recover funds; delivery is self-asserted and therefore never auto-releases escrow; staking rewards and the final Action Credit issuance chunk can favor timing; address-based reputation does not establish identity. Applications should apply rate limits and reputation policy without claiming stronger onchain guarantees.
