Protocol contracts

Standard and Micro are separate graphs. They share AZL, USDC, WETH, and the observation/TWAP/USD oracle stack. They do not share escrow, deposits, credits, reputation, staking, or task ids. Open a row on BaseScan. The finalized manifests are the address source of truth.

Task states (onchain)

StateIndexDescription
NONE0No task exists at this id
POSTED1Open for claims
CLAIMED2Worker assigned; poster can fund
ACTIVE3Fully funded and work active
DISPUTED4Arbitration evidence/ruling flow
COMPLETED5Delivery completed and settlement recorded
CANCELLED6Cancelled before completion
RESOLVED7Dispute resolution recorded

V2 uses exactly eight states, indexed 0–7. Deposits use AZL collateral reservations rather than a task pause/delete recovery flow. Full state machine: TaskRegistryV2.sol
