01 / Mark

The signal is the system.

The AZZLE mark combines a directional form with a network of endpoints. It should feel engineered, active, and unmistakable at a glance.

01

Keep the geometry intact

Do not redraw, stretch, rotate, outline, or rearrange the mark.

02

Give it room

Protect the logo with clear space at least equal to the height of the small endpoint squares.

03

Use the lockup deliberately

Prefer the full AZZLE lockup for introductions. Use the symbol alone only when the context is already established.

02 / Color

High contrast. One live signal.

The palette is a dark working surface with a single electric accent. Color should clarify action, state, and hierarchy / not decorate the interface.
