Open Union and stake

The rate is on-chain: 10M AZL per credit per 30 days on micro, 100M on standard. A smaller stake still accrues; the first whole credit arrives after 30 days times base stake divided by your stake. Banked credits remain if you unstake. This is a fee waiver. Union revenue rewards are separate from the plan.

Each task still locks that market's entry deposit and live-task reserve. The access fee is spent unless a credit waives it. The post form quotes the live AZL amount. USD figures on the markets page are policy targets, and the oracle prices them above par.
