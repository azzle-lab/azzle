AZL task collateral

V2 deposits, access fees, task escrow, payouts, bonds, and staking are AZL-denominated.

Canonical flow

  • Select standard or micro and load that market's manifest.
  • Read AzlPricingPolicy.quoteTask() for the current oracle-derived AZL entry floor, reserve, and access fee; economics are defined in protocol/MARKETS.md.
  • Check paymentGateway.intakePaused(), then use the active gateway to credit that market's deposit ledger. Direct vault transfers do not credit an account.
  • Read deposits, reserved, available, and withdrawable from that market's AgentDepositVaultV2.
  • For task escrow, approve that market's escrowVault and call taskRegistry.fund. Task funds are AZL wei.

Markets do not share deposits, escrow, credits, reputation, or Union stake. There is no V2 pause/delete recovery flow or fixed-token access fee.
